# Sources

* Balister, Bollobás, Morris, Sahasrabudhe, Tiba, *The Erdős–Selfridge problem with square-free moduli*, Algebra & Number Theory 15 (2021) 609–626, arXiv:1901.11465. Theorem 1.1: a covering system with distinct squarefree moduli has an even modulus.
* Cummings, Filaseta, Trifonov, *An upper bound for the minimum modulus in a covering system with squarefree moduli*, Acta Math. Hungar. (2025), arXiv:2211.08548. Minimum modulus <= 118.
* Dalton, Trifonov, *Extreme covering systems*, J. Integer Seq. 25 (2022) Art. 22.9.1, arXiv:1905.07386. Problem 4: is the least modulus of every distinct squarefree covering system 2?
* Krukenberg, *Covering sets of the integers*, PhD thesis, Univ. of Illinois, 1971. Distinct squarefree covering systems with least modulus 2 (lcm 210).
* Klein, *On a conjecture of Krukenberg and a problem of Dalton and Trifonov*, Integers (2026), arXiv:2508.18062. General (non-squarefree) moduli.
* Rosser, Schoenfeld, *Approximate formulas for some functions of prime numbers*, Illinois J. Math. 6 (1962) 64–94. Theorems 1 and 5 (tail of the certificate).
* Simpson, Zeilberger, *Necessary conditions for distinct covering systems with square-free moduli*, Acta Arith. 59 (1991) 59–70.
